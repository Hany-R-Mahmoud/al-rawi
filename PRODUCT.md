- Product: Al-Rawi, private paper-and-ink reader and unified Inbox for RSS, Google sources, explicit shares, and optionally native Android notification capture.

- Scope: owner-selected packages, default YouTube and Gmail, independent Android Notification Access, device-only native SQLite queue then IndexedDB v3 Inbox.

- Quality criteria: captured items remain searchable, editable, saved/done/archived without status reset; package filter occurs before extracting content; no notification content logging.
- Inferred: existing components and theme are the best visual baseline; device and owner acceptance are pending actual tests.
